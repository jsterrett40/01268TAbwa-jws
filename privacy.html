Ads

Aether Wing IV currently shows no ads on any platform. If we add ads in future, we will update this policy before they appear and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

NASA orbital data (Android and iPad apps)

About once a month, the Android and iPad apps ask NASA's JPL Horizons service (ssd.jpl.nasa.gov) for refreshed planet and moon orbit figures. The requests name only the planet or moon and contain no information about you, but, as with any internet request, NASA's servers can see your device's IP address. See NASA's privacy policy. If the request fails, the app uses the figures built into it. The web version does not make this request.

Purchases and tips

The Android app may offer an optional "Buy me a coffee" tip, which is a small purchase that unlocks nothing and simply says thank you. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play (or, if the game is ever listed there, the Apple App Store). We never see your payment details. We only receive confirmation from the store that a tip has been made.

Web version

The web version has no ads, no accounts and no purchases, and we do not set cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may record technical information such as your IP address in its server logs (see GitHub's privacy statement). The web version has a Support button that opens our page on Ko-fi in a new tab. Ko-fi (and its payment provider, Stripe) is an independent service with its own privacy policy; we do not receive your card details, only a confirmation of the tip and any name or message you choose to give.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
