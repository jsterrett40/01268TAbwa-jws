Privacy Policy

Aether Wing IV (iPad, Android and web) · Last updated: 7 October 2026

Aether Wing IV is a solar system explorer made by Hawthorn & Bramble ("we", "us"). This page explains what information the game uses and who else may receive it. In short: we don't ask you to create an account, and we don't collect your name, email address or location ourselves.

Who is responsible

Hawthorn & Bramble is the trading name of an individual developer based in the United Kingdom. You can contact us at hawthornandbramble@gmail.com.

What the game stores on your device

The game saves a few settings on your own device only: whether the music is on, whether you have removed ads, and your layout preferences. This information is not sent to us.

Ads (Android and iPad apps only)

The free apps show banner ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location, device and app details, and how you interact with ads. We do not receive this personal information. Google's use of it is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

  • Consent (UK and EEA): if you are in the United Kingdom or European Economic Area, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are used, through a consent form provided by Google. You can reopen your choices at any time using the Privacy button in the game's toolbar.
  • Device settings: you can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your phone's settings (on Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; on iPad: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
  • Removing ads: a one-time purchase in the app removes ads. If you buy it, the app stops requesting ads.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details. We only receive confirmation from the store that the purchase has been made, so that the app can remove ads.

Web version

The web version has no ads, no accounts and no purchases, and we do not set cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may record technical information such as your IP address in its server logs (see GitHub's privacy statement). The web version has a Support button that opens our page on Ko-fi in a new tab. Ko-fi (and its payment provider, Stripe) is an independent service with its own privacy policy; we do not receive your card details, only a confirmation of the tip and any name or message you choose to give.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal information about you, there is usually nothing for us to give you or delete. If you believe we hold information about you, or want to exercise your rights under UK data protection law (such as access, correction or deletion), email us and we will respond. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
